隱私權政策

最後更新：2026-10-05

我們不收集你的資料

BPack（以下稱「本網站」）是放在 GitHub Pages 上的靜態網頁，沒有後端伺服器。你的血壓紀錄只會存放在兩個地方：

  • 你自己 Google 雲端硬碟中的一份 Google 試算表（「BPack 血壓紀錄」，登入後由本網站建立）；
  • 你目前使用的瀏覽器的本機儲存空間（用於離線記錄與尚未同步的資料）。

網站作者無法存取上述任何資料。

Google 帳號權限

  • drive.file：只能存取本網站建立的檔案，或你親自選取的檔案；看不到你雲端硬碟中的其他內容。
  • openid、email：只用來在畫面上顯示你目前登入的帳號。

本網站對 Google 使用者資料的使用遵守 Google API Services User Data Policy，包括其中的 Limited Use 規定。資料不會傳送給任何第三方，也不會用於廣告。

無廣告、無分析、無 Cookie

本網站不放廣告，不使用 Google Analytics 或任何其他瀏覽量統計、分析或追蹤工具，也不設定任何 Cookie。除了 Google 登入與讀寫你自己的試算表之外，本網站不會連線到任何其他服務。

匯入與匯出

「匯出紀錄」會把你的紀錄下載成一個 JSON 檔到你的裝置；「匯入紀錄」可以讀取本網站匯出的 JSON 檔。這兩個動作都在你的瀏覽器裡完成，檔案不會上傳到任何地方。

匯入時你也可以選擇用 AI（例如 ChatGPT、Claude）整理舊紀錄：那是你自己把資料貼到你選擇的 AI 服務，本網站提供的提示詞只有格式說明，不會傳送、也看不到你的資料；該服務如何處理資料，由它自己的隱私權政策規範。

AI 分析（選用）

使用「統計 → AI 分析」時，你選擇期間內的紀錄會由你的瀏覽器帶到你選擇的 AI 服務（例如 ChatGPT、Claude），或複製到剪貼簿由你自行貼上。本網站不會傳送、也看不到這些內容；該服務如何處理資料，由它自己的隱私權政策規範。按下按鈕前可以先「查看實際會交給 AI 的內容」。

刪除資料

你可以隨時直接刪除雲端硬碟中的試算表；在本網站「設定」中登出會清除瀏覽器內的本機資料。你也可以在 Google 帳戶的第三方存取權 頁面撤銷本網站的授權。

非醫療器材

本網站僅供個人記錄，顯示的血壓分級與統計數字只是參考，不構成醫療診斷或建議。請勿未經醫師討論就依本網站的數字調整藥物。

政策變更

本政策如有修改，會更新本頁的「最後更新」日期。

聯絡

有任何問題，請到 GitHub 提出 issue。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-05

We do not collect your data

BPack ("the site") is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages with no backend server. Your blood-pressure readings are stored only in:

  • a Google Sheets spreadsheet in your own Google Drive ("BPack Blood Pressure Log"), created by this site after you sign in; and
  • your browser's local storage, for offline entries and data not yet synced.

The site author cannot access any of this data.

Google account permissions

  • drive.file: access only to files this site created or files you explicitly select. It cannot see anything else in your Drive.
  • openid, email: used only to show which account you are signed in with.

This site's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is never sent to third parties or used for advertising.

No ads, no analytics, no cookies

The site shows no ads, uses no analytics or tracking tools of any kind (not even an anonymous page-view counter), and sets no cookies. Apart from Google sign-in and reading and writing your own spreadsheet, the site connects to no other service.

Import and export

"Export readings" downloads your readings as a JSON file to your device; "Import readings" reads JSON files exported by this site. Both happen entirely in your browser; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

When importing, you may choose to let an AI (such as ChatGPT or Claude) tidy up old records. In that case you paste your own data into the AI service you pick; the prompt provided by this site contains only format instructions, and the site never sends or sees that content. The service's own privacy policy applies.

AI analysis (optional)

When you use Stats → AI analysis, the readings in the period you choose are taken by your browser to the AI service you pick (such as ChatGPT or Claude), or copied to your clipboard for you to paste. This site does not send or see that content; the service's own privacy policy applies. You can review exactly what will be shared before tapping a button.

Deleting your data

You can delete the spreadsheet from your Drive at any time. Signing out in Settings clears the data stored in this browser. You can also revoke this site's access on your Google Account third-party access page.

Not a medical device

This site is for personal record-keeping only. The blood-pressure categories and statistics it shows are for reference and are not medical diagnosis or advice. Never change your medication based on the site's numbers without talking to your doctor.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date on this page will be updated.

Contact

For any questions, please open an issue on GitHub.
